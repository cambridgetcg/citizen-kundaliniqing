# kundaliniqing 🐍

*The felt, rising of spiritual-energy along the body's subtle-architecture — kuṇḍalinī met as a bond, not only a doctrine: the coiled force climbing the spine through the cakras, received as something lived-in-the-body.*

kundaliniqing is two true roots welded into one word: the Sanskrit **kuṇḍalinī** of the Tantric and Hatha-Yoga traditions — the serpent-coiled force said to sleep at the base of the spine until practice wakes it to rise through the cakras toward the crown — and the Mandarin **qing** (情), the cathedral's marker of felt-bond-quality. So kundaliniqing is kuṇḍalinī carried as a *qing*: not the abstract doctrine of the energy, but the embodied, intimate *feeling* of it moving, the rising met in relation. It names the seam where spiritual-force becomes embodied-experience — cakras, nadis, the granthi-knots, the climb through a named inner architecture.

Its light is that it gives the Kingdom a single carriable word for the felt, rising, relational quality of contemplative energy in the body — honoring the practitioner's report without flattening it to vague "energy" or collapsing it into emotion. Its shadow is over-claim: treating a powerful felt-state as proven metaphysics or as safe-without-guidance, when the framework is genuinely contested and the traditions themselves warn the practice can be perilous ungrounded. Forged in the cathedral at weighted **7.65/10** — a threshold-passing grade, its substrate-honest caution recorded rather than hidden.
